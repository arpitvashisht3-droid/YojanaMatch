YojanaMatch 🇮🇳

AI-Powered Government Scheme Discovery & Assistance Platform

YojanaMatch is an AI-powered platform designed to help marginalized and underserved entrepreneurs in India discover and access government subsidy, loan, and financial assistance schemes they may be eligible for.

Government schemes are often spread across multiple portals, difficult to understand, and inaccessible to people facing language, literacy, or information barriers. YojanaMatch simplifies this process by allowing users to describe their situation in plain language or voice and receive personalized scheme recommendations.

⸻

🎯 The Problem

Millions of entrepreneurs across India may qualify for government schemes but struggle to access them because of:

* Fragmented government scheme information
* Complex eligibility criteria
* Language and literacy barriers
* Lack of awareness about available schemes
* Difficulty understanding required documents
* Dependence on intermediaries and middlemen
* Difficulty navigating government application portals

⸻

💡 Our Solution

YojanaMatch allows users to simply describe their situation.

For example:

“I am a 25-year-old woman from Haryana. I belong to the OBC category and want to start a small tailoring business. I need around ₹3 lakh.”

YojanaMatch analyzes the user’s requirements and provides personalized, ranked government schemes they may be eligible for.

Users can discover:

* 🧠 AI-powered scheme matching
* 🏆 Ranked scheme recommendations
* ✅ Clear eligibility explanations
* ❌ Reasons for ineligibility
* 💰 Subsidy and loan information
* 📑 Required document checklists
* 🗣️ Multilingual and voice-based interaction
* 🔗 Direct links to official application portals
* 🛡️ Protection against misleading intermediaries

⸻

🚀 Key Features

🧠 Smart Eligibility Matching

Matches users with schemes based on factors such as:

* Age
* Gender
* Social category
* Location
* Income
* Occupation
* Business type
* Investment requirements

🔍 Explainable Eligibility

Instead of simply showing Eligible / Not Eligible, YojanaMatch explains:

Why you qualify → Why you don’t qualify → What is missing → What you can do next

🗣️ Multilingual & Voice Access

Users can interact with the platform using natural language or voice in Indian languages, making government information more accessible to people with limited digital literacy.

🏆 Smart Scheme Ranking

Instead of giving users a huge list of schemes, YojanaMatch prioritizes the schemes that are most relevant to their individual situation.

📑 Document Readiness

Users can understand which documents are required for a particular scheme and identify what they still need before applying.

💰 Financial Assistance Discovery

Users can easily understand the potential:

* Loan amount
* Subsidy
* Financial assistance
* Own contribution
* Other applicable benefits

📋 AI-Assisted Project Reports

Entrepreneurs can receive assistance in preparing structured business/project reports required for certain government schemes.

🛡️ Safe Application Guidance

YojanaMatch directs users toward official government application channels, reducing dependence on middlemen and unofficial sources.
